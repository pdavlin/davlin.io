I want to say from the jump that I really did not enjoy this (because it was really very scary and I don’t like scary movies) but I did find this to be an interesting bit of incel-era horror commentary on top of a fairly traditional monkey’s paw story. What dude actively getting friendzoned hasn’t wished they could just create love? But… what about the consequences…

An interesting thought experiment but it does sort of feel undercut by the filmmakers’ desire to elaborate on the mechanics and rules of the world. I think an Annihilation-style ambiguous ending would’ve been better!
