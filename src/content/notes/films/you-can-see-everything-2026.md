Lots of Fielder’s work centers around “hey look at this freakin weirdo” and there’s certainly a TON of that in this film but more than anything else he’s done I feel like there is a note of something far more delicate and respectful here than I expected. But as usual it’s an incredible ride, maybe his craziest to date. I’m excited to see what Nathan Fielder comes up with next.

Watched at a PACKED Film Streams screening which was really cool.
