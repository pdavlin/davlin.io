My expectations were low coming in so I was really wowed by the quality of the animation. Plot's paper thin, and there's not really any humor here, and I don't really understand why Chris Pratt was anywhere near this. It's like they were just trying to cram in as many references as possible in 90 minutes.

Mario games have so much... style? joy? personality? ambition? so it's strange to see this project play everything so safe for mass market appeal.
