There are moments in this--particularly in the first half hour--when it feels like it's all taking forever and simply not doing anything interesting, and there are many, many moments in the film where it seems like Spielberg is close to losing his touch, pulling on the same tired narrative threads that he's depicted in a bunch of films. And it's all a bit... boomer optimism, the idea that if something came out on broadcast television that the world would all simultaneously notice and care.

But in the last 20 minutes or so it all comes together to one of those truly magical moments that it seems like only Spielberg can pull off; the John Williams score perfectly capturing the wonder and awe of an interaction that feels like it’s happening in front of you. Nothing quite like that.
