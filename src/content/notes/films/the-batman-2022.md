I’ve been publicly dumping on this movie for months, mostly because the grimdark DC aesthetic is tiresome and trying. This movie is mostly no exception to that but I’m glad I watched it, at least for the discourse.

Paul Dano rocks. He’s not on screen that much but all his scenes are good even if he looks like a latex geek. Really compelling. Robert Pattinson pretty well clears the bar as Batman, I think it’s the same vibe as Edward in Twilight but that really works! Colin Farrell is great too. The music is really good and the action scenes are excellent. It doesn't suffer from the occasionally wonky editing and action choreography of the Nolan movies. It doesn’t spend a bunch of time arguing about “hope” and “you have to become an idea” and “the soul of Gotham,” which is nice. We are also finally—finally—spared a Wayne family death scene. 

BUT

It’s too long by about a third. It becomes almost a chore to sit through at points because you can practically feel the minutes ticking by. There’s way too much going on in the plot, so it feels very self indulgent. The third act feels totally flat. There is a scene toward the end of the film that is clearly setup for a sequel, which grinds my gears almost to the nub. It feels like this movie really wants to say something about society, but really only in a “yaaaas” kind of way. 

I dunno, I wish I enjoyed it more. It would be nice if DC could come out with a Batman or Superman film that isn’t relentlessly self-serious. It’s a lot to sit through.
