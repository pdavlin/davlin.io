I’m normally not a Melissa McCarthy fan but this is definitely a movie made by people who actually love spy movies. Good action sequences, a decent balance of comedy and plot, and lots of thoughtfully placed jokes. Second act drags a bit and the Nancy character grinds jokes to a halt every time she’s on screen but otherwise I had a great time with this.
