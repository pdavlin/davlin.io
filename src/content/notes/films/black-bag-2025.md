Slightly less clever than it tries to be but I appreciate that its core theme ends up being fairly understated. Really hate the halation effect in the cinematography and the way that practical lighting is clearly picked to accentuate it consistently bugged me. Worth the watch just for Fassbender and Blanchett are titans and not even the Bridgerton Duke or 90s James Bond can pull your attention away.
