I wanted to re-watch this after seeing The Batman mostly because I needed something in the background while I worked today but also because I wanted to make some comparisons.

Heath Ledger is still the primo superhero villain, this performance is absolutely iconic for good reason. I also think Bale does a good job playing a dignified-on-the-outside, petty-and-fragile-on-the-inside Bruce Wayne which is a nice contrast with Pattinson's approach. Some of the practical stunt work remains impressive even in contrast to all of the insane CGI development that's happened in the past 15 years.

There are definitely parts of this that haven't held up so well. In particular some of the action choreography and staging are really murky and confusing. I also think it's less consistent, tone-wise, than the new movie. I have always felt like Aaron Eckhart is not really that compelling as an actor, and his Two-Face is pretty lukewarm for me.

More than anything, it's hard to watch this now and not get a little bit depressed at the ways that a lot of our domestic extremism problems are mirrored so strongly, and that the movie pretty openly embraces the idea of a mass-surveillance approach to countering that threat; those parts of the movie don't land as well for me now in 2022 as they did when I was a teenager.

This is a seminal bit of superhero film and it's definitely a product of its time. I'm not as big a fan of this now as I was even a few years ago, but you really can't dispute how important this movie is, culturally.
