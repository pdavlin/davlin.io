Perhaps best viewed as a retelling of Wuthering Heights instead of an adaptation. In a lot of ways this feels like it adopts the language and rationalization of therapy speak; it clearly views the two main characters as being so shaped by their individual and shared trauma that they cannot help but degrade and destroy their surroundings. Without the Brontë's ending (a generous term for the entire last half or so of the book, lol) Heathcliff and Catherine each ultimately are vindicated by the film for their behaviors. Heathcliff's biggest sin is that he wasn't there in time, Catherine's is that she was too proud to admit how she really feels. Yes, they destroyed Edgar's and Isabella's lives, but those two were... ultimately pretty into that, in this telling.

Not enough people are comparing Emerald Fennell to Zack Snyder. This is a lot like any of his films; if you put in on muted at a really artsy party people would love it. But beyond a series of beautiful frames, I think this is less than the sum of its parts.

Charli soundtrack is great. GOAT
