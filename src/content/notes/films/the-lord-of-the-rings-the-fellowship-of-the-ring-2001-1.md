Every so often you just have to watch these films to remember just how good they are. This is a perfect time capsule for the early 2000s. They couldn’t have made this film sooner—it’s a technical marvel and its effects hold up nicely even now. They _wouldn’t_ have made this movie later, without watering it down in some truly unbearable ways.
