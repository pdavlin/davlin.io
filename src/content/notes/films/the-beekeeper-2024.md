It’s *John Wick* mad libs. It’s a Hallmark Movie, but for dads. It’s the apotheosis of the post-Taken era of action movie plots, it contains an outrageous number of monologues about bees, it has several scenes composed of shots that were clearly done in one take. Its plot is remarkably stupid. It has some of the worst CGI and camera work I’ve ever seen. 

I loved it. I’ll never watch it again.
