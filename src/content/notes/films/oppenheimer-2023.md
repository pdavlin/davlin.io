A visual spectacle in IMAX and a well-deserved, long-overdue feature film role for Cillian. Tense and unnerving all the way through, and I feel it’s a bracingly honest portrayal of a complicated and flawed man. I’m glad Nolan chose not to play it all as a “great man of history” film. 

It’s long, frequently obtuse to figure out in what order things are happening, and the sheer number of famous cameos is disruptive to the action on screen. I am going to go on record and say that I’m glad they portrayed Einstein as an ambling soft spoken goofball. 

I won’t give this five stars with Nolan’s sound mixing preferences, though. For god’s sake it’s two dudes talking in an empty room why the FUCK can’t I understand what they’re saying!!! It’s past time for us to be able to understand people in Nolan’s films. I can’t find an excuse for a director of his stature to still be putting us through this.
