I wanted to watch this so I could appreciate the HBO series a little more but there’s a critical difference: I’m enjoying the HBO show and this was torture to sit through.

Moore’s Watchmen is such a painfully cynical story but it’s “important” and “gritty” and “inspired all kinds of comic books since the 80s” so I’ll give it a pass, I guess.

Zack Snyder did a good job adapting the imagery of the novel into this movie, but I frankly don’t like his style of filmmaking, particularly the way he completely overdoes it on the violence when he doesn’t need to. 

Summary: I’m pretty sure I could do a better Nixon impression than the guy in this movie. He didn’t even do a cheek shake!!
