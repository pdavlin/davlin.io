Watched this while I was on my bike training. I tried not to like it, but I did enjoy myself even though the movie is way too long. Writers do a fairly good job of letting even a giant cast of minor characters see some little storylines. 

Rocket Raccoon is some of the best CGI in the industry right now. My thoughts go out to the world-class animators who had to put anthropomorphic forest animal abuse on film for an unfair wage.
