Went into this thinking it would be a fun romp/romance in 1980s gym culture and was surprised to find a gripping, intense movie. Kristen Stewart is... thoroughly herself, here, but playing a loner gym manager fits her style well. Katy O'Brien surprises, playing a manic mix of intense emotions almost every scene.

It's thoroughly A24's house style and I think really undercuts its own ending by going metaphorical at the last minute, but the journey here is great even if the destination isn't what you expected. Glad I saw it.
