I should've seen this in IMAX but my parents' home theater was fine. It's a pretty dang impressive movie as a technical endeavor, and it's fun to watch a bunch of planes fly around set to dad rock. I also thought it was well-casted, and actors here are doing pretty well considering a lot of the roles are pretty one-note.

Outside IMAX, it's a 90-minute training montage and a 30 minute dogfight padded with a lot of platitudes about how "the pilot matters" and extremely nonspecific allusions to "threats" and "kill canyons," or whatever. Disappointing to see a film about the US Military pull so many punches here but that's what it is, I guess.
