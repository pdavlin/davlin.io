Newland lives in a prison of his own design. He resents Ellen for not marrying him but won’t help her get a divorce that would enable her to; he resents May _for_ marrying him even though she gives him the chance to leave; he resents high society but won’t flaunt its rules; he resents New York but won’t move away. His advances on Ellen are obvious to May but he isn’t bold enough to reject May. Wealth and privilege have sanded him down to a smooth polish; the pregnancy news barely moves him.

Who else lacks the emotional intelligence to end a relationship even though he’s unhappy? The average Love Island contestant!!

On a technical side I loved this. Costumes, sets, food, music, cinematography, editing. All lavishly attended to, each contributing to the luxury of high society (maybe even better than Gilded Age, one of my fav period piece shows) but also to a general claustrophobia and tension in Newland’s life.

Loved this. Special experience to see it today and it’ll be on my mind for a while.
