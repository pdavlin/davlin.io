feels more like a deeply horny showcase for Keoghan than something intended to be a good film on its own. in that regard it’s pretty satisfying. it feels a lot like someone smashed Joker into a love story, and the accompanying tonal shift feels like whiplash. 

the way Keoghan plays Oliver is very engaging—his eyes dart around every room he’s in. is it insecurity, or a watchful eye toward being caught in a lie? either way, I’m sure he’ll win an award or two for it.
