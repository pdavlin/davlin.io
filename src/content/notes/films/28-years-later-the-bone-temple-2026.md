_This review may contain spoilers._

I think the fact that this film did away with a bunch of weird novelty filmmaking techniques is really rewarding for me. A gorgeously filmed and edited work. I typically really don't like music drops in movies but several of the ones in this film landed for me and at least one Iron Maiden moment is... the best I've ever seen in a movie.

Like the heights of the 28 [unit of time]s Later series I think this has a profound emotional core that both transcends and perfectly complements its setting. Choosing to focus on Kelson through the film allows for a greater depth of meaning to its individual threads; his kindness and his compassion toward all life, even the infected, even the evil, is so disarming both to the viewer and to other characters in the film. It certainly leads to his death; it certainly saves Spike. Part of what makes Fiennes' performance so compelling here is that he perfectly portrays a brief moment where his own performance as the devil scares himself a little even though it's so obviously campy to the audience.

Everyone here is having a blast. I had a blast. Can't wait to watch again.
