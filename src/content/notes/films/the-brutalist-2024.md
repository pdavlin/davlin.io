Excellent cinematography and score. Obsessive about its own design and direction, sometimes to the point of excess. But I was, constantly, in awe of its striking visual direction. I think the performances here are good across the board. Brody feels mostly like a foil against the other characters in the movie during the first half and then sort of develops into more of a distinct personality in the second half.

I like the idea of the structure of the movie as presented: the first half being the promise of America for an immigrant, and the second half being the reality of being taken advantage of by a system that only seeks to exploit you. I think during the second half it begins hitting you over the head with its extended American Dream allegory like Bugs Bunny walloping Daffy Duck with a giant mallet.
