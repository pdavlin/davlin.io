I think this is really the first superhero movie I can remember that feels pulled indirectly from a comic book—colors, motion, framing, and editing that feels a lot like panels put to screen. Think it’s pretty telling that they can’t make a “just a spider-man movie” that doesn’t also exist to set up the next two or three movies. Feels like lots of what I’ve read about this one glosses over the setup.
