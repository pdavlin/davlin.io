slightly cooled on this after a second viewing. In the whole movie we see him bite exactly three people and there’s this pervasive sense of the movie telling us that Nosferatu is infinitely powerful but not always showing it. Most of the fun here is the ✨vibes✨, and that’s okay! It’s a gorgeous movie and a fun watch. But I’m not sure that Nosferatu himself lives up to the hype.
