Really pretty impressed that you can get so much mileage out of the Work of Journalism in a film but admittedly a little disappointed that this one feels like it has no payoff because there's another couple years(?) of reporting that gets summarized in a 30-second montage at the end.

I love the way that Woodward's process is laid out visually, the attention to his notes and construction in interviews is great.
