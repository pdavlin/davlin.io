Much more by-the-numbers as zombie movies go. I don’t think the world building approach is bad—obviously a lot of attention was paid to the hard science fiction aspect of explaining how a settlement can work and how the infection can cross boundaries without detection. But once you start going down that rabbit hole you’re asking to have all the plot holes pointed out. Why isn’t anyone guarding the carrier once she’s discovered? Why turn out all the lights and make it impossible to identify the infected before you start shooting? Who cares??? Once you dedicate your film to explaining the vectors of the infection your characters become minor details. You have to explain the exact parameters of how the Infection jumps to Europe--everything else is just a contrivance.

28 DAYS LATER was a Vibes Masterpiece but visually the sequel is a lot more homogeneous with other mid-00s fare. Adoption of the Greengrass-style shaky cam is done poorly here. Other than The Kiss it didn’t make much of an impression on me.
