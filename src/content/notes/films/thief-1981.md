Now I can see why people have been recommending I watch this one for years. Absolute vibes masterpiece with precise attention to visual and process details. They should've legally required all movies made in the 80s to be scored by Tangerine Dream.

The core trope, I guess, of any story following a criminal is what happens when his eyes get bigger than his stomach. In Frank's case his childlike pursuit of the American Dream drives him to work with a criminal capitalist. The film led me to believe that Frank's risk comes from the job itself; that Frank might get busted by cops or caught in the act, but s a thief, he's impeccable. Frank's alliance with Leo is ultimately his undoing; guess you just can't trust capital!

Overall this does feel a bit like a prototype for Heat but I'll probably have to rewatch that to get a better idea.
