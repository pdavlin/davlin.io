Watched right after we finished the new Apple TV miniseries and I think this is better in almost every way. Yet more proof that the 2026 TV miniseries format is just stretching movies out to 8-10 hour runtimes. This version pulls off somehow being really weird but also stifling and tense. Different core message than the show but same shape overall, and I think more compelling because it’s altogether more ambitious but mostly because De Niro is a real freak!
