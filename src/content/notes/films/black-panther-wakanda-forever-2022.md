Watched this while biking indoors. I appreciate that Coogler is able to resist the Marvel house style of undercutting serious moments with terrible humor so a lot of the beats in this one do land. But it’s hard not to feel like this movie is unfocused, compared to the first Black Panther, and it strains under the weight of having to set up the new Iron Man and of course whatever Julia Louis-Dreyfus is supposed to be.
